    Controls

    • W or up — charge the boss
    • S or down — back away, eyes still on the boss
    • A/D or left/right — circle the boss; the camera swings with you
    • You are locked on: the hero always faces the boss, and letting go stops you dead
    • Left click or J — sword
    • Right click or K — gun
    • Space or Shift — dodge roll, briefly invulnerable
    • Q — drink a flask
    • Esc — back to the menu

    Stamina gates everything. Swing and roll until it runs dry and you're a sitting duck.
